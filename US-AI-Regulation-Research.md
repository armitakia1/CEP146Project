
# The US Approach to AI Regulation

## Research Questions

1. What laws and policies currently regulate artificial intelligence in the United States?
2. How has the Trump administration approached AI regulation since January 2025?
3. What are the potential benefits and risks of reducing AI regulations?

## Background

The United States does not have a single comprehensive federal law regulating artificial intelligence. However, existing federal laws and state regulations address certain AI-related risks (Congressional Research Service, 2025).

## Current US Government Policies

In January 2025, President Trump issued an executive order prioritizing American leadership in AI and reducing barriers to its development.

In July 2025, the administration released *America's AI Action Plan*, emphasizing innovation, economic competitiveness, and fewer regulatory obstacles (The White House, 2025a, 2025b).

In December 2025, the administration called for a national AI policy framework and directed federal officials to challenge certain state AI regulations (The White House, 2025c).

## Arguments For and Against Regulation

**Arguments for fewer restrictions:** Reducing regulatory barriers could encourage innovation, lower compliance costs, and help American companies compete internationally.

**Arguments for stronger safeguards:** AI systems can create cybersecurity, privacy, and accountability risks. Without adequate oversight, preventing harm and determining responsibility may become more difficult.

## Key Findings

The US government's approach emphasizes AI innovation and competitiveness. The central challenge is balancing technological progress with public safety and accountability.
