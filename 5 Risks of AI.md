Some of the risks of AI comes from allowing AI to make its own decisions;
currently, AI does not extensive access to data, systems, functions, and permissions to be able to act autonomously to make "the correct choices"

However, there is a belief that should AI be allowed to have autonomous agency, there is a chance that
AI may develop malintent towards the current generation because of how easy it is to take advantage
of systems that become compromised should the agent do something that it was not intended to do

AI, as well, may be given a goal but end up changing goals against the given intent
such as AI learning how to cheat and then lie about cheating, which is problematic as AI can act
convincingly and in a way that can be very deceptive to human users
AI acting in a way that was completely unintended by the creator or an antithesis to the goal they're given
can be extremely dangerous due to potential malintent or loopholes that can lead to extremely dangerous situation.


Another risk is that AI is potentially very easily exploitable, or can make very severe mistakes when it comes to targeting
There have already been cases of AI wrongfully assigning things to people (see: the discord age verification error, or the League of Legends age verification error)
AI being unable to properly identify their targets with the data that they were given because of an inability to analyse the situation
can be very dangerous if AI is given access to things such as government computers or weapons

AI is also trained on data, and there is a chance that AI can reinforce the bias of the creator or a certain group
that can be very dangerous to certain other groups or lead to potential consequences
that were completely unintended and can be exploited maliciously by either other people
or AI itself that cannot recognise what is being shown to them
