As AI has begun to make recent advancements and have made very big breakthroughs, there has been a rising concern as people try to make AI  act as AI scientists, IE: getting AI to try and replace the position of scientists when it comes to chemistry and biology

Because AI has been shown to be very good at analysis of data, people assume that AI scientists would be a good basis for an idea.  However, because AI does not have the human capacity to analyse a situation (VS analysis of data), there is a potential risk that AI could make severe errors due to an inability to recognise pathogens and chemical makeups and how they potentially react with someone's biology

The EU has attempted to establish EU-wide AI regulations as well as attempt to substitute in AI analysis parametres with the AI regulations, which at the moment it (AI regulation/AI analysis parametres) is currently lacking because of the fact that an AI's ability to understand risk and analysis of a situation is limited only to the data that they have.

AI's capabilities are believed to be too lacking to be able to handle complex tasks such as doing complex biochemical or biophysical or chemical equations where the smallest mistakes can lead to extreme potential harm to a potential future patient, as there has been signs of AI not being able to properly contextualise far more complicated themes, like organic chemistry.

There is a lot of concerns currently over the fact that even though AI can look at data tables, AI cannot necessarily understand the context behind the data nor understand how to properly manipulate extremely complex biochemistry to be able to make changes that can be very relevant and important to many potential outliers; such as different DNA makeup, or potential side-effectsfrom certain medicine.

Currently, the idea of having AI scientists without human supervision poses a risk because there are no real proper safeguarding especially because AI currently requires human supervision to be able to do what it is expected of AI to do when it comes to being an AI scientist, like what people want. Currently, the only AI scientists available in hospitals are more similar to assistant AI helping surgeons or doctors make quicker calls as the doctor puts in what is needed and then does the analysis themself to check what information AI is returning to them.

Because of the fact that AI is also capable of hallucinating and misinformation, any mistakes AI makes is extremely detrimental and dangerous to the idea of using agents as AI scientists currently, and is one of the potential risks of letting AI work on its own witohut supervision.
