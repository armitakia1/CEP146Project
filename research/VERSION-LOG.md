## How to comment

Open the pull request for the branch `research-ai-liability` and go to the Files changed tab. Find `research/RESEARCH-DRAFT.md`, hover over a line and click the blue plus sign to leave a comment. The Suggest changes icon lets you propose exact wording. When you're done, choose Review changes and pick Comment, Approve or Request changes. Bigger ideas for the video can go in Discussions.

A few things worth checking as you read: whether every claim has a source in `SOURCES.md`, whether anything is stated more strongly than its source supports, and whether it's clear enough for a three-minute video.
